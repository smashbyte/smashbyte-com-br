Our first title

Alaria

Alaria Online is an old-school fantasy MMORPG that we plan to keep running for years. It keeps the danger and exploration of classic online RPGs and adds guilds, contracts, player housing and an economy driven by the players themselves. The in-game store helps pay for development. It does not sell power.

"Power is not bought. It is built."

What we do

Game development

Server infrastructure

We run our own game servers, databases and backend services in the cloud, with monitoring, alerts and backups.
